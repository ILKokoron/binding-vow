Verdicts and judge reports

The three judges, three different rules

  • judge1, the Literalist: approves only if the proof file's SHA-256 matches the committed hash exactly.
  • judge2, the Formalist: approves if the proof is reachable and a hash was committed; checks the form, not the content.
  • judge3, the Skeptic: approves only if the proof is reachable, at least 512 bytes, and the hash matches.

Judges are paid from the separate verification fee for examining, never for passing. A failed verdict pays them the same. Full evidence reports for the two reference cases, vow-3 (passed) and vow-4 (failed and executed), ship with this page under reports/ and are linked from those verdicts.

Contract: on NEAR testnet. The NFT is non-transferable. It always dies: redeemed on success, executed on failure, burned on cancel.
